Rasterise the logo suite

Renders public/logo-suite/ds_app_icon.svg to the PNG and ICO sizes the app references. The SVGs are generated by scripts/generate-logo-suite.mjs; this is the manual step, because there is no rasteriser in the dependency tree.

  1. Serve the repo (npm run dev) and open this file at http://localhost:3000/../scripts/rasterize-logo-suite.html, or just open it from disk — it falls back to a relative path.
  2. Click Render & download. Four files land in your Downloads folder.
  3. Move them into the repo, replacing the existing files:
    favicon.ico · icon-192.png · icon-512.png → public/
    ds_app_icon.png → public/logo-suite/
