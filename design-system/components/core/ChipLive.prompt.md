Use `ChipLive` only where something is genuinely in progress — one of volt's three permitted meanings.

```jsx
<ChipLive size="sm">live</ChipLive>
<ChipLive>in progress</ChipLive>
```

The dot is full-chroma volt; the text is `--ds-brand-text`, which is the darkened olive on chalk and volt itself on graphite. Do not use this chip for "done" — that is the volt fill on a day pill.
