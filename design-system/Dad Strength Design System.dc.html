04 · rules

the rules sheet

01
volt is a fill
never an ink on a light ground. on chalk, volt as text becomes brand-text. full-chroma volt appears on light only as a solid fill with brand-ink on top.
02
one accent, earned
volt means exactly three things: live, earned, and the number you lift. nothing else gets it. if everything is volt, nothing is.
03
numerals are heroes
the load, the week, the streak lead every card. tabular always, -0.045em tracking, 0.92 line-height.
04
shadows, not borders
tiles float. borders are sparing — a recessed row uses fill to separate, not a line.
05
lowercase voice
direct, second person, no exclamation marks. mono eyebrows carry metadata; prose carries intent. prose is never mono.
06
inks never fade
an ink token is never shown at reduced opacity. text that recedes uses concrete instead.
07
no warning color
anything not fine or broken is prose, or low-emphasis danger. do not introduce amber.
08
six category axes
push, pull, legs, core, condition, general. desaturated, each its own ink on its own 10% tint. never one hue per label.
